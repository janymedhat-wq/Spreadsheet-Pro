Purpose

All the tools you need for
serious spreadsheet work

Excel costs too much, and lighter tools don’t feel familiar enough to stay in flow. That’s why I built Spreadsheet Pro.

The goal was a full-featured spreadsheet app that is completely free and open source. It’s built around the way you actually work with data: formulas, charts, and pivot tables, with the controls you need to get every number right.

Because it’s open source, you can download the project and add, remove, or change any feature to fit your needs.

  • Spreadsheet EditingEditable cell grid and formula bar, multiple worksheets with tabs, new workbooks and sheets, rename and close sheets, insert and delete rows and columns with formula references adjusted automatically, selection highlighting, context menus, unsaved-change indicators and discard confirmation.
  • Undo & Redo NewCtrl+Z and Ctrl+Y undo and redo almost everything: typing, formulas, formatting, sorting, inserting and deleting rows and columns, notes, fills and pastes. Each sheet keeps its own history, and the commands also live in the Edit menu and as Undo and Redo buttons in the Home tab’s Clipboard group. While the Draw tab is active, Ctrl+Z undoes drawing strokes instead. Moving or resizing a chart or picture cannot be undone yet.
  • Copy, Cut & Paste NewCtrl+C, Ctrl+X and Ctrl+V keep formulas working. References shift as you paste, so =A1*2 copied one row down becomes =A2*2, and formatting comes along too. One copied cell can fill a whole selected range, and cut moves the cells. Ctrl+Shift+V pastes values only. Text from Excel, Google Sheets or Notepad pastes in, and a picture on the clipboard pastes as a picture. Copied cells get a moving dashed frame, and Esc removes it.
  • Fill Handle & Auto Fill Options NewHold Ctrl while dragging the fill handle of a single 1 and you get 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, while a plain drag still copies the value. After any fill, a small Auto Fill Options button appears at the corner. It offers Copy Cells, Fill Series, Fill Formatting Only and Fill Without Formatting, and redoes the fill with the choice you make.
  • Fill > Series NewFind Fill > Series... under Home > Editing and in the Edit menu. It fills in rows or columns, linear or growth, with a step value and a stop value. Type 1, choose Series, set the stop value to 6, and it fills 1 to 6.
  • Fill Down & Fill Right NewCtrl+D and Ctrl+R fill down and fill right. They copy the top row or the left column over the selection, or copy from the cell above or to the left when only one cell is selected.
  • Formula EngineExpression-tree parser with arithmetic, comparisons, parentheses and percentages, cell references and ranges, 128 functions, a function search and insert dialog, built-in formula reference, lazy IF branches, clear errors (divide by zero, bad syntax, unknown function, invalid or circular reference), show-formulas mode, and relative and absolute references when filling. Math and stats: SUM, AVERAGE, MIN, MAX, COUNT, PRODUCT, MEDIAN, STDEV, VAR, ROUND, ABS, SQRT and more. Logical: IF, AND, OR, NOT, IFERROR, ISNUMBER. Text: CONCAT, CONCATENATE, LEN, UPPER, LOWER, TRIM, LEFT, RIGHT, MID. Lookup: VLOOKUP, MATCH, INDEX. Date and time: TODAY, NOW, YEAR, MONTH, DAY, DAYS.
  • Cell FormattingFont family (the font box lists every font installed on your computer, each shown in its own style) and size, bold and italic, text and background colours, horizontal alignment, borders, number formats (General, Number, Comma, Currency, Percentage, Scientific), 25% typed as 0.25, increase and decrease decimals, a Cell Styles gallery, and controls that reflect the selected cell.
  • Data ManagementCSV and Excel import and export, ascending and descending sort, multi-column custom sort, header-aware sorting, AutoFilter with searchable dropdowns, Remove Duplicates, Text to Columns, data validation (lists, whole numbers, decimals, text length) with alerts and invalid-cell highlighting, clear rules, a fill handle for numbers, dates, text patterns and formulas, double-click fill-down and undo for fills.
  • Pivot Tables & AnalysisPivotTables on a new worksheet with row and optional column grouping, Sum, Count, Average, Minimum and Maximum, grand totals, summary statistics, calculated columns such as [Qty]*[Price], Goal Seek (secant and bisection), and in-cell sparklines (line, column, win/loss).
  • Interactive ChartsColumn, Bar, Line, Area, Pie and Scatter charts from selected data, live updates when cells change, hover tooltips with exact values, a clickable legend to hide series, source-cell highlighting, drag to move and corner-drag to resize, edit type, title and range, right-click menus, delete, and charts saved in workbooks.
  • Drawing & AnnotationFreehand pen, highlighter, line, rectangle and ellipse tools, nine brushes, an eraser for single strokes or shapes, eight quick colours plus custom, adjustable thickness, filled shapes, Shift for straight lines, squares and circles, undo and redo, undoable Clear All, drawings that scale with zoom, saved in workbooks and locked on protected sheets.
  • Brushes NewOpen the Brushes gallery from the Draw tab by clicking it or by hovering for a moment, or click the live preview in the Brush group. Choose from nine brushes: Pen, Pencil, Marker, Calligraphy, Crayon, Spray, Watercolor, Dashed and Neon. Each card shows the tool and a sample stroke in your current colour and thickness, and your brush style is saved in the file.
  • Image SupportInsert PNG, JPG, GIF and BMP files, paste from the clipboard, select and move pictures, resize from a corner handle, Shift for free stretching, delete, and pictures saved in native .spx workbooks.
  • Storage & SecurityNative .spx format with Save, Save As and Open, multiple sheets, and values, formulas, formatting and pictures preserved. Optional AES-256-GCM workbook encryption with PBKDF2 key derivation and password-protected opening, worksheet protection with optional passwords (salted PBKDF2 hashes), restrictions on protected sheets, and support for older native formats.
  • Excel InteroperabilityImport and export .xlsx keeping sheets, values, formulas, cached results, fonts, colours, number formats, alignment and column widths. Imported dates become yyyy-MM-dd text, and the last Excel-calculated value is kept for formulas that use unsupported functions.
  • Autosave & RecoveryConfigurable AutoSave interval, a recovery-copy mode that leaves your original untouched, optional saving into the open workbook, crash-recovery copies with a prompt on restart, encryption preserved when saving encrypted files, and settings inside the app.
  • File Menu NewA full File menu opens as its own screen with a sidebar of Info, New, Open, Save, Save As, Print, Share, Export, Close, Account and Options, and a round back arrow at the top. Press Esc or click the arrow to go back to your sheet. Save As, Print, Share, Export, Close, Account and Options are large, easy-to-click rows.
  • Open & Recent Workbooks NewThe Open page has Recent Workbooks, Computer and Excel file (.xlsx) on the left. On the right, your recent workbooks are listed with their folders, and one click opens them. The app remembers your last 12 saved or opened workbooks and skips files that were deleted.
  • Info & Account NewThe Info page shows the file name and location, plus properties such as sheet count, size, last modified, password and read-only. Buttons for password, version history, save version and recover unsaved sit alongside. The Account page includes Check for Updates, and the Options page holds Appearance and AutoSave.
  • Developer Tools & MacrosA VBA-style macro editor and console running a custom VBA-subset interpreter: variables and Dim, For/Next with Step and Exit For, If/ElseIf/Else, Cells(r,c) and Range("A1:B3"), value, formula and formatting properties, ClearContents and Clear, MsgBox, Debug.Print, numeric, string and RGB helpers, workbook info, cell inspection and Show Formulas.
  • Large WorksheetsChoose 100,000, 500,000 or 1,048,576 rows, with row numbers generated on demand and storage that only keeps used cells. 200,000-row sheets have been tested through native and Excel-format save and load.
  • Formula Suggestions NewStart typing a formula and a suggestion list shows the matching functions, each with a short description. Pick one and it is inserted for you, so you can write formulas faster and with fewer typing mistakes, even when you do not remember the exact name.
  • Dark Themes NewChoose a light or dark theme and the whole application follows it. The theme is applied to the ribbon, the toolbars and status bars, the grid and the cell renderer, so no part of the window stays bright when you work in the dark.
  • Language Support NewA built-in language system (I18n) lets the interface appear in your own language. Choose English, French, Arabic or Spanish in the Settings window, and the ribbon, menus, dialogs and messages follow, including the newest buttons and brush names. You can change the default language at any time.
  • Settings & Font Size NewOne Settings window brings the main preferences together: theme, language and interface font size. Increase the font size for comfortable reading on high-resolution screens, or reduce it to fit more on the screen.
  • Interface & ProductivityA refreshed ribbon with Home, Insert, Draw, Data, Power Pivot, Developer, View and Help tabs that follows your theme, an animated loading screen, Java2D vector icons, tooltips, context menus, keyboard shortcuts, per-sheet zoom from 25% to 300% with mouse wheel and keys, adjustable column widths, double-click autofit, reset widths, status bar, sheet tabs, a selection frame that glides between cells with the keyboard, and scrolling that eases to a stop.
  • TechnologyJava Swing and Java2D on JDK 8 or newer with no external libraries, shell and Windows build scripts, and a custom spreadsheet model, formula parser, renderer, workbook I/O and charting engine.

Good to know. Excel import and export is partial: pictures, drawings, charts, filters and sheet protection stay in .spx files, and sparklines are not exported. The macro engine covers a subset of VBA, not the full language or Excel's macro environment.
