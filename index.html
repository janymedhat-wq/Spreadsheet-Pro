Purpose

All the tools you need for
serious spreadsheet work

Excel costs too much, and lighter tools don’t feel familiar enough to stay in flow. That’s why I built Spreadsheet Pro.

The goal was a full-featured spreadsheet app that is completely free and open source. It’s built around the way you actually work with data: formulas, charts, and pivot tables, with the controls you need to get every number right.

Because it’s open source, you can download the project and add, remove, or change any feature to fit your needs.

  • Spreadsheet EditingEditable cell grid and formula bar, multiple worksheets with tabs, new workbooks and sheets, rename and close sheets, insert and delete rows and columns with formula references adjusted automatically, selection highlighting, context menus, unsaved-change indicators and discard confirmation.
  • Formula EngineExpression-tree parser with arithmetic, comparisons, parentheses and percentages, cell references and ranges, 64 functions, a function search and insert dialog, built-in formula reference, lazy IF branches, clear errors (divide by zero, bad syntax, unknown function, invalid or circular reference), show-formulas mode, and relative and absolute references when filling. Math and stats: SUM, AVERAGE, MIN, MAX, COUNT, PRODUCT, MEDIAN, STDEV, VAR, ROUND, ABS, SQRT and more. Logical: IF, AND, OR, NOT, IFERROR, ISNUMBER. Text: CONCAT, CONCATENATE, LEN, UPPER, LOWER, TRIM, LEFT, RIGHT, MID. Lookup: VLOOKUP, MATCH, INDEX. Date and time: TODAY, NOW, YEAR, MONTH, DAY, DAYS.
  • Cell FormattingFont family and size, bold and italic, text and background colours, horizontal alignment, borders, number formats (General, Number, Comma, Currency, Percentage, Scientific), 25% typed as 0.25, increase and decrease decimals, a Cell Styles gallery, and controls that reflect the selected cell.
  • Data ManagementCSV and Excel import and export, ascending and descending sort, multi-column custom sort, header-aware sorting, AutoFilter with searchable dropdowns, Remove Duplicates, Text to Columns, data validation (lists, whole numbers, decimals, text length) with alerts and invalid-cell highlighting, clear rules, a fill handle for numbers, dates, text patterns and formulas, double-click fill-down and undo for fills.
  • Pivot Tables & AnalysisPivotTables on a new worksheet with row and optional column grouping, Sum, Count, Average, Minimum and Maximum, grand totals, summary statistics, calculated columns such as [Qty]*[Price], Goal Seek (secant and bisection), and in-cell sparklines (line, column, win/loss).
  • Interactive ChartsColumn, Bar, Line, Area, Pie and Scatter charts from selected data, live updates when cells change, hover tooltips with exact values, a clickable legend to hide series, source-cell highlighting, drag to move and corner-drag to resize, edit type, title and range, right-click menus, delete, and charts saved in workbooks.
  • Drawing & AnnotationFreehand pen, highlighter, line, rectangle and ellipse tools, an eraser for single strokes or shapes, eight quick colours plus custom, adjustable thickness, filled shapes, Shift for straight lines, squares and circles, undo and redo, undoable Clear All, drawings that scale with zoom, saved in workbooks and locked on protected sheets.
  • Image SupportInsert PNG, JPG, GIF and BMP files, paste from the clipboard, select and move pictures, resize from a corner handle, Shift for free stretching, delete, and pictures saved in native .spx workbooks.
  • Storage & SecurityNative .spx format with Save, Save As and Open, multiple sheets, and values, formulas, formatting and pictures preserved. Optional AES-256-GCM workbook encryption with PBKDF2 key derivation and password-protected opening, worksheet protection with optional passwords (salted PBKDF2 hashes), restrictions on protected sheets, and support for older native formats.
  • Excel InteroperabilityImport and export .xlsx keeping sheets, values, formulas, cached results, fonts, colours, number formats, alignment and column widths. Imported dates become yyyy-MM-dd text, and the last Excel-calculated value is kept for formulas that use unsupported functions.
  • Autosave & RecoveryConfigurable AutoSave interval, a recovery-copy mode that leaves your original untouched, optional saving into the open workbook, crash-recovery copies with a prompt on restart, encryption preserved when saving encrypted files, and settings inside the app.
  • Developer Tools & MacrosA VBA-style macro editor and console running a custom VBA-subset interpreter: variables and Dim, For/Next with Step and Exit For, If/ElseIf/Else, Cells(r,c) and Range("A1:B3"), value, formula and formatting properties, ClearContents and Clear, MsgBox, Debug.Print, numeric, string and RGB helpers, workbook info, cell inspection and Show Formulas.
  • Large WorksheetsChoose 100,000, 500,000 or 1,048,576 rows, with row numbers generated on demand and storage that only keeps used cells. 200,000-row sheets have been tested through native and Excel-format save and load.
  • Interface & ProductivityRibbon with Home, Insert, Draw, Data, Power Pivot, Developer, View and Help, Java2D vector icons, tooltips, context menus, keyboard shortcuts, per-sheet zoom from 25% to 300% with mouse wheel and keys, adjustable column widths, double-click autofit, reset widths, status bar, sheet tabs and scrolling.
  • TechnologyJava Swing and Java2D on JDK 8 or newer with no external libraries, shell and Windows build scripts, and a custom spreadsheet model, formula parser, renderer, workbook I/O and charting engine.

Good to know. Excel import and export is partial: pictures, drawings, charts, filters and sheet protection stay in .spx files, and sparklines are not exported. The macro engine covers a subset of VBA, not the full language or Excel's macro environment.
